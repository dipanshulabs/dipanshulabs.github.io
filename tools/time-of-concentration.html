Time of Concentration Calculator

Kirpich Formula

T_c = 0.0195 × L^0.77 × S^−0.385
T_c = time of concentration (minutes) L = hydraulic length (m) S = slope = H / L (m/m)

FAA Formula

T_c = (1.8 × (1.1 − C) × L^0.5) / S^(1/3)
T_c = time of concentration (minutes) C = runoff coefficient (dimensionless) L = overland flow length (m) S = slope (%)

Bransby-Williams Formula

T_c = 0.057 × L / (A^0.1 × S^0.2)
T_c = time of concentration (minutes) L = main stream length (m) A = catchment area (km²) S = average stream slope (m/km)
T_c is used to determine the design storm duration for the Rational Method. When T_c is less than the minimum storm duration in your IDF data, use the minimum storm duration as the design duration instead.
