Orifice (Free Flow)

Q = C_d × A × √(2gH)
Q = discharge (m³/s) C_d = discharge coefficient A = orifice area (m²) H = head above orifice centre (m) g = 9.81 m/s²

Rectangular Sharp-Crested Weir

Q = (2/3) × C_d × L × √(2g) × H^(3/2)
Q = discharge (m³/s) C_d = discharge coefficient (~0.611) L = weir crest length (m) H = head over crest (m)

V-Notch (Triangular) Weir

Q = (8/15) × C_d × √(2g) × tan(θ/2) × H^(5/2)
Q = discharge (m³/s) θ = notch angle (degrees) H = head above vertex (m)

Broad-Crested Weir

Q = C_d × L × H × √(g × H)
Q = discharge (m³/s) C_d = discharge coefficient (≤0.848) L = crest length (m) H = upstream head (m)
Discharge coefficients are empirical and vary with geometry and flow conditions. Always verify against applicable design standards and physical model tests for critical structures.
