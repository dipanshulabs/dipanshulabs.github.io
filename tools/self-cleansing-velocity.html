Manning's Equation — Full Bore

V = (1/n) × R^(2/3) × S^(1/2)
Q = V × A = V × π(D/2)²
R = D / 4
V = flow velocity (m/s) n = Manning roughness coefficient R = hydraulic radius = D/4 for full circular pipe (m) S = pipe slope (m/m) Q = flow rate (m³/s) A = pipe area = π(D/2)² (m²)
All calculations assume full-bore flow conditions. For partially full pipe flow, apply appropriate depth-ratio corrections. Always verify the minimum velocity requirement against the applicable local authority drainage standard.
