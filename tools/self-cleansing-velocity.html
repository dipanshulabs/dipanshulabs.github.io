Manning's Equation — Full Bore

V = (1/n) × R^(2/3) × S^(1/2)
S_min = [ V_min × n / R^(2/3) ]²
V = flow velocity (m/s) n = Manning roughness coefficient R = hydraulic radius = D/4 (full pipe) (m) S = pipe slope (m/m) S_min = minimum slope for self-cleansing (m/m)
Self-cleansing must be satisfied at the design dry weather flow (DWF) for foul sewers. For stormwater sewers, check at peak design flow. Always confirm the required minimum velocity against your applicable local authority design standard.
