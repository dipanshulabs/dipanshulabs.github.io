Method

V = (1/n) × R^(2/3) × S^(1/2)
V_full = Q_full / A
Full-bore velocity calculated from Manning's equation Design flow velocity = Q_design / A (full pipe) Check: V_design ≥ V_min for self-cleansing Also check V_design ≤ V_max (typically 3.0 m/s for concrete)
Self-cleansing must be satisfied at the design dry weather flow (DWF) for foul sewers and at peak flow for stormwater systems. Always confirm against applicable local authority design standards.
