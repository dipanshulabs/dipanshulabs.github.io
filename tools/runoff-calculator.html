Rational Method Formula

Q = (C × i × A) / 360
Q = peak discharge (m³/s) C = runoff coefficient (dimensionless, 0 to 1) i = rainfall intensity (mm/hr) A = catchment area (ha) 360 = unit conversion constant Derivation: 1 mm/hr × 1 ha = 10,000 m² × (1/3,600,000) m/s = 1/360 m³/s
Assumptions: Rainfall duration equals or exceeds the catchment time of concentration T_c. Rainfall is uniformly distributed. Best suited to catchments < 200 ha. For larger catchments, use hydrograph methods (e.g. unit hydrograph, SCS CN).
