Manning's Equation

V = (1/n) × R^(2/3) × S^(1/2)
Q = V × A
V = mean flow velocity (m/s) Q = discharge (m³/s) n = Manning roughness coefficient (dimensionless) R = hydraulic radius = A / P (m) A = cross-section flow area (m²) P = wetted perimeter (m) S = channel bed slope (m/m)
Manning's equation assumes steady, uniform, fully turbulent open channel flow. It is not suitable for partially full pipe flow without correction factors. Always verify n values against the channel material and local design standards.
