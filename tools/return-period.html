Formulas

AEP (%) = (1 / T) × 100
T = 100 / AEP (%)
Risk = 1 − (1 − 1/T)^n
T = return period (years) AEP = annual exceedance probability (%) n = design life (years) Risk = probability of exceedance at least once in n years
