Flow Regime Reference

Regime Reynolds Number
LaminarRe < 2,000
Transitional2,000 ≤ Re ≤ 4,000
TurbulentRe > 4,000

Thresholds above are for pipe flow. For open channels the transitional range may vary. Re > 12,500 is generally considered fully turbulent in most channels.

Formula

Re = V × D / ν
Re = Reynolds number (dimensionless) V = flow velocity (m/s) D = hydraulic or pipe diameter (m) ν = kinematic viscosity (m²/s)
