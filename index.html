Driven by science,

Operating across international field programmes, I collaborate with governments, NGOs, and research institutions to translate complex climate data into actionable, community-centred engineering strategies.

What I Offer

Services & expertise

From early-stage feasibility studies to detailed design and monitoring programmes, I provide independent expert guidance across the full water and climate engineering lifecycle.

Hydrological Assessment

Catchment analysis, flood frequency estimation, low-flow analysis, and drought vulnerability assessments using state-of-the-art modelling tools.

Climate Risk & Adaptation

Translating IPCC scenarios into project-level climate risk profiles and designing adaptive management plans for long-lived infrastructure.

Sustainable Drainage Design

SuDS masterplanning, green roof design, bioretention systems, and permeable paving schemes integrated into wider urban greening strategies.

Environmental Monitoring

IoT sensor network design, remote sensing analysis, and real-time dashboards for continuous water quality and ecological health monitoring.

Stakeholder Engagement

Facilitating participatory design processes, co-producing solutions with communities, and translating technical findings for non-specialist audiences.

Research & Technical Writing

Peer-reviewed publications, technical reports, grant applications, and policy briefs connecting rigorous research to real-world decision-making.
