About Me

Driven by science,
guided by water

I am an environmental and water resources engineer with a focus on hydrology, hydraulics, and climate-resilient water systems. My work spans the full cycle of water — from rainfall modelling and river hydraulics to sustainable drainage design and nature-based flood management.

Based in the Netherlands, I collaborate with research institutions, governmental bodies, and engineering teams to develop data-driven solutions that balance environmental performance with practical infrastructure needs in a changing climate.

What I Offer

Services & expertise

From hydrological assessments and hydraulic modelling to climate adaptation strategies and nature-based design — I offer independent technical expertise across the full water engineering spectrum.

Hydrology & Hydraulics

Catchment hydrological modelling, 1D/2D hydraulic analysis, flood frequency estimation, and low-flow studies for infrastructure design and planning.

Climate Adaptation

Translating IPCC climate scenarios into project-level risk profiles and designing adaptive water management plans for long-lived infrastructure.

Sustainable Drainage

SuDS masterplanning, green infrastructure design, bioretention systems, and permeable paving schemes integrated into urban water management strategies.

Water Quality & Monitoring

Environmental monitoring network design, remote sensing analysis, and data dashboards for continuous water quality and ecological health assessment.

Nature-Based Solutions

Design and assessment of nature-based flood management, constructed wetlands, river restoration, and green–blue infrastructure for multi-benefit water outcomes.

Research & Technical Writing

Peer-reviewed publications, technical reports, grant applications, and policy briefs connecting rigorous water science to real-world engineering decisions.
