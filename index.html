About Me

Built on data,
designed to last

I am a hydraulic and stormwater modelling engineer holding the Dutch-recognised academic title ir. (Ingenieur) and a Graduate Member of CIWEM. My work spans IFC-stage wet utilities design, hydraulic modelling and flood risk assessment for major urban masterplans, road corridors and railway infrastructure across the Middle East and Africa.

I specialise in gravity-based network optimisation, multi-utility coordination within BIM environments, and authority submissions for large-scale drainage systems. Currently based in the UAE, I combine international field experience with a strong academic foundation in water resources engineering.

Selected Work

Featured projects

Trunk Sewer · IFC Design

Dubai Creek Harbour — Major Trunk Sewer

Led IFC-stage hydraulic design of a trunk sewer system up to 1,400 mm diameter serving a large mixed-use waterfront development. Eliminated a proposed 450 L/s pump station through gravity-based network reconfiguration, improving long-term system sustainability. Coordinated multi-utility BIM clash resolution and led authority submissions with regulatory bodies.

📍 Dubai, UAE SewerGEMS · Civil 3D · Navisworks
Road Drainage · HEC-RAS

40 km Road Drainage Corridor — Riyadh

Designed and validated a full surface drainage system for a 250 ha catchment corridor, achieving zero surface flooding with surcharge control. Validated for 1:5 to 1:50-year storm events across at-grade and tunnel sections. Conducted bridge hydraulic modelling, flood depth estimation, scour assessment and sedimentation analysis using HEC-RAS. Performed DEM-based hydrological modelling and flood routing.

📍 Riyadh, Saudi Arabia HEC-RAS · InfoWorks ICM · ArcGIS
Railway · 1,200 km Corridor

Lobito–Luena Railway Corridor — Angola

Hydraulic design and analysis of 907 crossings across a 1,200 km railway corridor, comprising 120 bridges (1:100-year), 343 box culverts and 444 circular culverts (1:50-year). Conducted catchment modelling, flood simulation and erosion risk assessment. Contributed to a long-term climate resilience strategy ensuring operational continuity during extreme rainfall events.

📍 Angola, Africa HEC-HMS · HEC-RAS · ArcGIS
Urban Masterplan · 210 ha

Bloom Living — Mixed-Use Drainage Masterplan

Technical lead for wet utilities design across a 210 ha mixed-use urban masterplan. Directed hydraulic modelling strategy for stormwater, sewer and potable water networks. Validated for 1:5-year storms and verified under 1:10-year events, maintaining surcharge within compliance thresholds. Managed grading coordination and authority submissions.

📍 Abu Dhabi, UAE SewerGEMS · Civil 3D · InfoWorks ICM

What I Offer

Services & expertise

From concept-level drainage strategy to IFC-stage hydraulic design and authority compliance — I bring international consultancy experience to water infrastructure projects of all scales.

Hydraulic Network Design

IFC-stage design of stormwater and sewer networks. Gravity optimisation, trunk sewer sizing, pump station elimination, and multi-utility coordination using SewerGEMS and InfoWorks ICM.

Flood Risk Assessment

Hydraulic modelling and validation for 1:5 to 1:100-year storm events. Surface flooding analysis, surcharge assessment, and extreme event compliance for urban drainage and transport infrastructure.

Bridge & Culvert Hydraulics

Bridge hydraulic modelling, flood depth estimation, scour assessment and sedimentation analysis using HEC-RAS. DEM-based catchment modelling and hydrological flood routing for crossing design.

Urban Masterplan Drainage

Drainage strategy and hydraulic design for large-scale urban masterplans. Catchment delineation, runoff analysis, network layout and phased IFC delivery coordinated with transportation and structural disciplines.

Authority Submissions & Compliance

Technical preparation and defence of IFC submissions to municipal and regulatory authorities. Design compliance review, shop drawing verification, and hydraulic performance sign-off for construction-stage delivery.

Climate-Resilient Infrastructure

Integrating climate adaptation into drainage and water infrastructure design. Long-term resilience strategies for extreme rainfall, flood routing, and sustainable gravity-based system optimisation.
