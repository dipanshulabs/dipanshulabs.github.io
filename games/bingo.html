How to Play
  • Your card has 25 squares (5×5) with random numbers
  • B column: 1–15 · I: 16–30 · N: 31–45 · G: 46–60 · O: 61–75
  • The centre FREE square is already marked
  • Press "Call Number" or use Auto Call to draw numbers
  • Mark called numbers on your card
  • Win by completing any row, column or diagonal
  • Full House = all 25 squares marked
  • "New Card" gives you a fresh card. "Reset" restarts everything
